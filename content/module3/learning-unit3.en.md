title: "Tuples: Immutable Data"
summary: Tuples are structures similar to lists, but with the main characteristic of being immutable, meaning their elements cannot be modified after creation. In this unit, you will learn what tuples are, how they are defined, and how to access their values, as well as the scenarios in which it is appropriate to use them, especially when you want to protect constant data or represent fixed information. You will also explore the practical differences between lists and tuples in terms of security, performance, and usage in real-world programs.
